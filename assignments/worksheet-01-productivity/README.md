## Q1: Lakefront Manufacturing (bicycle tires)

1,000 tires/day · labour 400 h × $12.50 · raw material 20,000 lb × $1 · energy $5,000 · capital $10,000 (all per day)

- **(a)** Labour productivity = 1,000 ÷ 400 = **2.5 tires per labour-hour**
- **(b)** MFP = 1,000 ÷ (5,000 + 20,000 + 5,000 + 10,000) = 1,000 ÷ 40,000 = **0.025 tires per $**
- **(c)** Energy bill cut by $1,000 → 1,000 ÷ 39,000 = 0.02564 → (0.02564 − 0.025) ÷ 0.025 = **+2.56%**

## Q2: Susan Williams job shop

8 workers × 45 h = 360 labour-hours. 52 flawed "seconds" sold at $90, 80 garments sold at $198.

- Output value = 52 × 90 + 80 × 198 = 4,680 + 15,840 = $20,520
- Labour productivity = 20,520 ÷ 360 = **$57.00 per labour-hour**
- The seconds still count because they were sold. The $10/h wage isn't needed: the question asks per labour-**hour**.

## Q3: Charles Lackey bakery

1,500 loaves/month at 2.344 loaves per labour-hour, 160 h per worker per month. Demand +25% → 1,875 loaves.

- **Workers:** now 1,500 ÷ 2.344 = 640 h = **4 workers** (this is the "4 workers" in the key). New: 1,875 ÷ 2.344 = 800 h = 5 workers → **add 1 worker**.
- **(a) Labour option** ($8/h): 1,500 ÷ (640 × 8) = 0.293 → 1,875 ÷ (800 × 8) = 0.293 loaves per $ → **0% change**
- **Blender option** (+$100/month, still 640 h): 1,875 ÷ (5,120 + 100) = 0.359 → **+22.6%**, so the blender is the better decision
- **(b) No blender** (utilities $500, labour $8/h, ingredients $0.35/loaf):
  - Before: 1,500 ÷ (500 + 5,120 + 525) = 1,500 ÷ 6,145 = 0.2441
  - After: 1,875 ÷ (500 + 6,400 + 656.25) = 1,875 ÷ 7,556.25 = 0.2481
  - Change = **+1.6%** (1.65% unrounded)

## Q4: Two AC units (weekly, Tk)

| Unit | Labour | Material | Energy | Output | Price |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| A (1.5 ton) | 10 | 100 kg | 150 kWh | 300 pcs | Tk 50,000 |
| B (2 ton) | 15 | 200 kg | 200 kWh | 200 pcs | Tk 70,000 |

Material Tk 100/kg · energy Tk 20/kWh · 9 h/day × 5 days · wage Tk 1,000 per labourer per day

- **(a)** Labour productivity: A = 300 ÷ (10 × 9 × 5) = **0.67 pcs/LH** · B = 200 ÷ (15 × 9 × 5) = **0.30 pcs/LH**
- **(b)** A: 15,000,000 ÷ (50,000 + 10,000 + 3,000) = **238.10 Tk/Tk** · B: 14,000,000 ÷ (75,000 + 20,000 + 4,000) = **141.41 Tk/Tk**
- **(c)** Whole company = 29,000,000 ÷ 162,000 = **179.01 Tk/Tk** (not the average of A and B, which would be 189.76)
- **(d)** Energy productivity: A = 300 ÷ 150 = 2 pcs/kWh, B = 200 ÷ 200 = 1 pcs/kWh → **Unit A is higher**. Every other version (Tk/kWh, Tk/Tk, pcs/Tk) gives the same answer.
