# Quiz 1 quick prep: Productivity & Forecasting

The short version: learn these, then try the 12 questions.

## Productivity: 6 things to know

1. **Productivity = Output ÷ Input**
2. **Growth % = (New − Old) ÷ Old × 100**. For example, 80 → 84 is 4 ÷ 80 = **5%**.
3. **Single-factor** uses one input, usually **labour**. **Multifactor** uses several inputs, all in **$**.
4. Count **good units only**, and use the **same time period** for everything.
5. **Efficient** = doing things right. **Effective** = doing the right thing.
6. The 3 key factors for improving productivity are **labour, capital and management**.

## Forecasting: 8 things to know

1. **Error = Actual − Forecast**
2. **Naive forecast** = last period's actual.
3. **Moving average** = the average of the last *n* periods. **More periods = smoother but slower.**
4. **Exponential smoothing:** New F = Old F + α × (Actual − Old F). **Bigger α = reacts faster.**
5. Forecasts get **less accurate the further ahead** they look. **Group forecasts** are more accurate than single items.
6. **Qualitative** = opinions (e.g. **Delphi**: anonymous experts, repeated rounds). **Quantitative** = numbers (time-series, regression).
7. **Seasonality** = a pattern that repeats within a year. **Trend** = long-term up or down.
8. **MAD** = average size of the error. **MSE** punishes **big** errors. **MAPE** = error in %. **Tracking signal** spots **bias**.

## 12 easy practice questions

1. Productivity is: (a) output × input (b) output ÷ input (c) input ÷ output
2. 3 workers make 60 units in 2 hours. Labour productivity = ? (a) 30 (b) 20 (c) 10 units per labour-hour
3. Productivity goes from 50 to 55. Growth = ? (a) 5% (b) 10% (c) 55%
4. 100 units made, 4 defective, in 4 hours. Productivity = ? (a) 25 (b) 24 (c) 26 units/hour
5. Inputs in multifactor productivity must be: (a) all labour (b) in the same unit, like $ (c) in hours
6. A naive forecast for May uses: (a) April's actual (b) the yearly average (c) expert opinion
7. The 3-month moving average of 10, 20 and 30 is: (a) 30 (b) 20 (c) 60
8. Old forecast = 100, actual = 120, α = 0.5. New forecast = ? (a) 110 (b) 120 (c) 100
9. A bigger α makes the forecast: (a) smoother (b) react faster (c) unchanged
10. Ice-cream sales rise every summer. This is: (a) trend (b) seasonality (c) random
11. Actual = 90, forecast = 100. Error = ? (a) +10 (b) −10 (c) 190
12. Which measure is a **percentage**? (a) MAD (b) MSE (c) MAPE

> Answers: 1 b · 2 c (60 ÷ 6) · 3 b (5 ÷ 50) · 4 b (96 ÷ 4) · 5 b · 6 a · 7 b · 8 a (100 + 0.5 × 20) · 9 b · 10 b · 11 b (90 − 100) · 12 c
